# Reba - demo video script

Target: 3 minutes. Read slowly. Short sentences.
Recorded as a SCREEN RECORDING with voice over. iPhone, microphone ON.
[SCREEN] = what to have on the phone screen. "Say" = the words.

---

## 1. WHO (15 sec)

[SCREEN: the Reba home screen, still. Do not tap yet.]

Say:
> Hello. My name is David Mugisho.
> This is Reba. It is Kinyarwanda for "look."
> It is a five minute eye check for community health workers in Rwanda.
> It works offline, on a cheap Android phone.

---

## 2. THE PROBLEM (25 sec)

[SCREEN: stay on the home screen. Do not tap. Let it rest while you talk.]

Say:
> Two things take a child's sight in Rwanda.
> Retinoblastoma is a cancer of the eye. Found early, the child lives. Missed, the child dies.
> Amblyopia is an eye that never learns to see. After age seven, the loss is permanent.
> Neither one hurts. The child never complains.
> The person who sees the child first is a community health worker.
> Today, they have nothing to look with.

---

## 3. APP DEMONSTRATION - starting (15 sec)

[SCREEN: open the language menu, change the language, come back. Then tap new screening.]

Say:
> This is Reba. There is no network in this village. None is needed.
> The health worker chooses the language. Kinyarwanda, English, French, German.
> Then: new screening.

---

## 4. APP DEMONSTRATION - consent (20 sec)

[SCREEN: the consent screen. Scroll it slowly so the whole script is seen.]

Say:
> First, consent. This script is read out loud to the family.
> It says what we look for. It says this is not a diagnosis.
> It says two photographs will be taken, and they stay on this phone.
> It says the family can stop at any time.
> The mother says yes. We continue.

---

## 5. APP DEMONSTRATION - calibration (25 sec)

[SCREEN: the calibration screen. Tap plus and minus so the outline visibly grows and shrinks.]

Say:
> Now something simple that makes the test real.
> Right now I am holding my ID card against the phone, standing up.
> I adjust this until both sides line up with the card.
> Every ID card in the world is exactly the same size. Fifty four millimetres across.
> So now the app knows how big a millimetre is on THIS screen.
> The letters that come next are a true physical size. Not a guess.
> This is why the same test gives the same answer on a small phone and a big one.

---

## 6. APP DEMONSTRATION - the vision test (35 sec)

[SCREEN: the E screen. Tap through several. Let the viewer SEE the E shrink line by line.
 Pause a moment on each E before tapping, so the rotation is visible.]

Say:
> The child is standing three metres away from this phone. One eye covered.
> A letter E appears, turned a random way.
> The child points which way the legs face. The worker taps that arrow.
> [tap, pause, tap, pause]
> Three letters per line. Each line the child gets right, the next one is smaller.
> Watch it shrink.
> Two mistakes on one line, and that eye is finished.
> What comes out is a measurement. Six over twelve. Six over eighteen.
> Not an impression. A number.

---

## 7. APP DEMONSTRATION - the photographs (25 sec)

[SCREEN: the camera screen. Frame an eye, take the shot, let the captured photo appear.
 TEST THIS IN REHEARSAL: check the camera preview is not black in the recording.]

Say:
> Now the photographs. Darkest corner of the room. Flash on.
> One photograph of each eye, at arm's length.
> You know the red eye effect in family photos.
> That red is the flash bouncing off the back of the eye.
> It means the path is clear.

---

## 8. WHY IT CAN BE TRUSTED (30 sec)

[SCREEN: open the saved record and show the two eye photos side by side,
 with the measured reflex numbers under them.]

Say:
> When something blocks that path, a cataract, or a tumour, the pupil comes back white.
> Doctors call it leukocoria. It is the sign that finds retinoblastoma.
> Usually a parent spots it in a photo. By chance. Too late.
> Reba measures it on purpose. It compares the two eyes.
> Same phone, same flash, seconds apart. So skin tone and light cancel out.
> The patient is their own control.
> We tested it on clinical photographs of two children with retinoblastoma.
> The healthy eyes measured zero point four seven five, and zero point four seven six.
> The sick eyes measured zero point three eight one, and zero point three seven five.
> Both were flagged. Neither healthy eye was.
> Anyone can run that check in our repository.

---

## 9. APP DEMONSTRATION - the result (25 sec)

[SCREEN: the result screen. Scroll down to the family script, then open the referral slip.]

Say:
> The result. One of three bands, big enough to read outdoors.
> Refer. Recheck. Or no signs today.
> Reba never says healthy. The mildest answer is "no signs today,"
> and it says in the same breath that this is not the same as healthy eyes.
> Below it, a script to read to the family. And a referral slip.
> Five minutes, from start to finish.

---

## 10. APP DEMONSTRATION - EjoChat (20 sec)

[SCREEN: the explain screen. Type a real question. Wait for the answer, show it.
 Needs network, so use Do Not Disturb, NOT airplane mode.]

Say:
> After the check, the parent is worried and has questions.
> This uses the EjoChat API. The worker types the family's question.
> The answer comes back in Kinyarwanda, grounded in this screening.
> It is not allowed to diagnose. Every clinical question goes back to the clinician.
> With no signal, the written script still works.

---

## 11. HONESTY + CLOSE (25 sec)

[SCREEN: the result screen, on the line that says there is no trained model.
 End on the history list, showing the saved screenings.]

Say:
> One thing I want to be clear about.
> There is no trained model yet. The app says so on the result screen.
> A model needs thousands of eye photographs, labelled by ophthalmologists,
> taken on phones, in villages. That dataset does not exist for Rwanda.
> Because nobody had the tool to collect it.
> Reba is that tool. Every screening already captures a pair.
> Reba does not diagnose. It flags the eyes a nurse should examine.
> The decision is always theirs.
> Thank you.

---

## Checklist before recording

- [ ] MICROPHONE ON: Control Centre, long press the record button, tap the mic until it is red
- [ ] Do Not Disturb ON (not airplane mode, EjoChat needs the network)
- [ ] Phone charged, brightness at maximum
- [ ] 15 second test recording: check the voice is there AND the camera preview is not black
- [ ] Do one FULL screening without recording, check it behaves
- [ ] Real ID card in your hand for the calibration step
- [ ] A dark corner for the flash photos
- [ ] Quiet room, no fan, no traffic
- [ ] Speak slowly. Slower than feels natural.
- [ ] Keep tapping SLOWLY. On a screen recording, fast taps are impossible to follow.
